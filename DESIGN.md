## Brand & Style

This design system embodies care, trust, and contemporary ease for modern pet parents and service providers. It pairs the clinical reassurance of veterinary expertise with the warmth and emotional vitality of pet companionship. 

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate** reliability with **Approachable Consumer Marketplace** dynamics:
- **Clean and Pristine:** Generous white space and crisp structural grouping eliminate visual clutter, letting high-quality imagery and vital data (ratings, delivery time, veterinary credentials) stand out.
- **Friendly Geometry:** Generously rounded corners soften high-density catalog grids, evoking gentleness and comfort without feeling juvenile or cartoonish.
- **Vibrant Precision:** Vibrant emerald botanical accents denote wellness, organic vitality, and safety, grounded by dependable technical blue signifiers for trust metrics, verified badges, and actionable interactions.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for daylight clarity, healthcare reassurance, and marketplace conversion.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0E9F6E` - Emerald):** The foundational brand color representing wellness, care, and vitality. Used for primary CTAs, active states, cart triggers, and core navigation highlights. Darkened to `#057A55` for high-contrast interactive hovers and accessible text; softened to `#D1FAE5` for soothing notification backdrops and badges.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Royal Trust Blue):** Reserved for technical trust cues, service booking flows, verified clinic tags, distance indicators, and interactive informational callouts. Supported by `#3B82F6` and the airy `#EFF6FF` container wash.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#F9FAFB` provides a comfortable, soft off-white backdrop that prevents screen glare.
  - `Card / Surface Elevate`: `#FFFFFF` delivers clean contrast and crisp separation for product tiles.
  - `Text Primary`: `#111827` delivers high legibility and sharp contrast.
  - `Text Secondary & Borders`: `#4B5563` for descriptive copy; `#E5E7EB` for soft, neutral dividing lines.
- **Semantic Accents:** Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) for customer review stars and urgency flags; Rose (`#EF4444`) strictly for cancellations, stock alerts, and health warnings.

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, humanistic headers with **Inter** for rational, high-density data rendering.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings):** Features subtle curved terminals and expansive counters that project friendliness and optimism without losing professional authority. Used across collection titles, hero banners, merchant names, and pricing callouts.
- **Inter (Body, UI, Metadata):** Engineered for ultra-clear legibility across complex catalog elements such as nutritional tables, multi-tier pricing, clinic opening hours, reviews, and ingredient lists.
- **Micro-Copy & Tags:** Labels under 14px utilize `fontWeight: 600` or `700` with slight positive tracking to ensure instant scannability on badges, distances, and status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 8-point geometric scale underpins all marketplace screens, balancing rich inventory density with readable separation.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout, 24px gutters, max-width bounded at 1280px with centered margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, 16px gutters, 24px margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 12px gutters, 16px margins, optimized for single-hand vertical feed scrolling and touch targets.
- **Component Flow Rhythm:**
  - Tightly grouped metadata (e.g., star icon alongside rating score, delivery distance dot) uses `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px).
  - Internal card padding uses `space-md` (16px) to preserve edge breathing room.
  - Sections, category rails, and vendor carousels decouple with `space-xl` (32px) on mobile and `space-2xl` (48px) on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean surfaces layered with soft ambient shadow casting rather than dense, harsh outlines.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F9FAFB` base. Serves as the continuous viewport plane.
  - **Level 1 (Cards, Stacks, Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surface lifted with an ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`. Outlined with a hairline border: `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
  - **Level 2 (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns, Segmented Tabs):** `0px 10px 20px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)` with a primary-tinted border hover state (`#0E9F6E` at 30% alpha).
  - **Level 3 (Modals, Sticky Bottom Sheets, Floating Cart Bar):** `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.1), 0px 10px 10px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)` to detach transactional dialogues from interactive page contexts.
- **Glassmorphic Navigation Bar:** Sticky top search headers employ a frosted white substrate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) maintaining context during vertical catalog browsing.

## Shapes

The design system embraces welcoming, approachable geometry that reinforces pet wellness and human warmth.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, nested micro-buttons, and table groupings.
- **Card & Container Radius (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Standard for product cards, merchant shop displays, appointment time selectors, and modular banners.
- **Featured Surface Radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Used on promotional campaign heroes, quick-reorder cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Applied to badges (ratings, distances, pet tags), category filter chips, search input bars, and circular icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full `#0E9F6E` emerald background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height of 44px (touch friendly) with `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg` (8px). Hover transitions to `#057A55` with subtle scale feedback.
- **Secondary / Action:** Soft `#EFF6FF` background with `#2563EB` text and borderless padding, offering clean affordance for reordering and secondary actions.
- **Outline:** Crisp 1px `#E5E7EB` border, white surface, `#111827` text, shifting to a green border on interaction.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Built with `rounded-full`, height 36px, `space-md` horizontal padding.
- **Unselected:** White fill, `#E5E7EB` border, `#4B5563` label.
- **Selected:** `#D1FAE5` emerald tint fill, `#0E9F6E` border, `#057A55` bold label, optional clear icon.

### Cards (Product & Service Provider)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded with 16px corner radii, light 1px border (`#E5E7EB`), soft ambient shadow.
- **Media Header:** Aspect ratio 1:1 (products) or 16:9 (clinics/groomers) with top corner radius matching the parent.
- **Floating Floating Badges:** Absolute positioning over image corners using pill containers:
  - Distance: White backdrop with secondary blue glyph (e.g., `📍 1.2 km`).
  - Ratings: Amber star glyph with bold score (e.g., `★ 4.9 (120)`).
- **Body & Footer:** Product name in `headline-sm`, subtitle/brand in `body-sm`, price bolded in `headline-md` alongside a circular "+" primary action button.

### Form Inputs
- Rounded to 8px or 9999px (search bar).
- 44px minimum touch target, subtle neutral border (`#D1D5DB`), background `#FFFFFF`.
- Focus state brings an active 2px ring in primary emerald (`#0E9F6E`) with soft shadow spread, avoiding harsh browser defaults.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px dimension, 6px radius for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `#D1D5DB` perimeter border. Checked: Solid `#0E9F6E` fill with white checkmark glyph.

### Pet Profile Badge (Domain-Specific)
- Compact avatar badge featuring the pet's photo, name, species icon, and breed in a warm `#F3F4F6` pill. Clicking toggles custom catalog filtering tailored to the selected animal.
